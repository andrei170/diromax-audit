The three things that are hardest to get, real five-star reviews, real prices and real before-and-after photos, are already there. Almost every failure is about those being hidden.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (20 Oct 2026)
AGoogle reviews
⭐⭐ 5.0 stars from 61 reviews on the Diromax Google listing. This is the strongest thing the business has online, and most local rivals have fewer (see Competitors).
A-Starting prices on the website
⭐ Eight services each show a “starts from” price on the services page (windows £15 up to roofs £200). None of the five competitors we read shows a price list on its home page.
BPage titles and descriptions
Home page title names the services and “Anglesey, Gwynedd & North Wales”, and there is a written description. Contact and services pages are titled for search too.
DGoogle listing set-up
⚠ The listing exists, with 236 photos, the category “Pressure washing service” and 01248 305 007 as its phone. But Google places it in the hills north-west of Bala, about 40 miles from Cemaes Bay (map position 52.985, -3.764). That usually means the address is hidden and the service area is set very wide.
FQuote form
⚠ One form, on the contact page only, with 8 required fields including email, job address and a “Branch Selector” (North Wales, Cheshire, Other). No form on the home or services pages.
FWhatsApp link
⚠ A WhatsApp icon is on every page, but the link is wa.me/4407464464511. WhatsApp's own instructions say to drop the 0 after the 44, so this may not open a chat. Worth tapping on the call.
FMobile layout
⚠ At phone width the main heading runs off the right edge of the screen (“Cleaning” and “North” are cut off), and the first screen has no menu, no phone number and no quote button.
FPages per service and per town
🔴 One services page for all eight services, and no town pages. There are 73 blog posts, and the newest is dated 30 May 2025.
FShowing up on Google Maps
🔴 In three Google Maps searches Diromax came 12th for “pressure washing Anglesey”, 13th for “gutter cleaning Holyhead”, and was not in the first 13 for “roof cleaning Bangor Gwynedd”. Firms with fewer reviews came first.
FPaid ads
⚠ Meta: one gutter-cleaning video ad ran from 28 November 2025 to 24 January 2026, and nothing is running now. Google: no ads ever, for diromaxexteriorcleaning.co.uk, in the UK.
FStructured data for Google
🔴 The only structured data is a basic “WebSite” label. Nothing tells Google this is a local business, where it is, or that it has 61 five-star reviews.
FAI answers (ChatGPT-style search)
🔴 We asked an AI web-search assistant two questions about gutter and roof cleaning near Cemaes Bay. It named Diromax both times, then said it has “zero reviews”, because the only review count it found was a FreeIndex listing with none.
FReviews shown on the website
🔴 The site shows no reviews and no star rating, only a “See Our Reviews on Google” link. The 61 five-star reviews are invisible to anyone on the site.
FTap-to-call
🔴 No tap-to-call link on any of the six pages we read. 07464 464511 and 01248 305 007 are plain text in the footer, so a phone visitor has to copy the number.
FTracking
🔴 No Google Analytics, Google Ads or Meta pixel found in the page code of six pages. ⚠ Wix can add some tools in ways that do not show there, so confirm in the Wix dashboard.

⚠ Not graded: page speed (Google's PageSpeed tool refused our requests that day). Left out rather than guessed.
