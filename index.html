The Three Findings

1. Your best selling points are hidden

Veteran-owned, honest upfront pricing, real before-and-afters. Three things customers love, and the current site barely mentions any of them. That's the cheapest fix there is: show what you've already got.

2. Reviews and Google are where the jobs come from, and both are quiet

Someone searching "gutter cleaning Anglesey" taps the top map result with the most stars. Nothing's asking your happy customers for a review, and none are shown on the site, so the thing that wins the call isn't happening.

3. The good news: you've capacity and a story that sells

You can take 5-10 more jobs a week, and "ex-forces, honest prices, photos every job" is a marketing gift. Put that in front of the local market with a proper site, reviews and a bit of visibility, and the diary fills.
