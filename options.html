Two ways to start

Sized for a one-person business. Both start with the free fixes, and neither needs you to spend on ads until the basics work. ⚠ Every price on this page is a placeholder for Andrei to set on the call.

Option A · Get found and get called

⚠ £ price: Andrei to set once, then ⚠ £ price: Andrei to set a month

  • Google listing fixed: service area, phone, services, prices
  • New website: tap-to-call, WhatsApp, quote form on every page, reviews and prices up front, a page per service and per main town
  • Tracking on every call and form
  • Missed-call text back
Option B · Get found, get called, get them back

⚠ £ price: Andrei to set once, then ⚠ £ price: Andrei to set a month

  • Everything in Option A
  • Keep It Clean plans set up, with reminders when each customer is due again
  • Automatic review request after every job
  • Every quote chased until it is a yes or a no
  • Seasonal Meta and Google ads when you are ready, managed for you
Then each month
Growing only when you say so
  • Step 1: management only, no ad spend. ⚠ £ price: Andrei to set a month
  • Step 2: add a small seasonal ad budget, paid by you straight to Meta or Google. ⚠ £ price: Andrei to set a month
  • Step 3: more services and more towns, once the numbers show it works. ⚠ £ price: Andrei to set a month
What neither option includes
  • Ad spend. Paid by you to Meta or Google, on your own card.
  • A promised number of jobs, enquiries or rankings. Anyone who gives you one before seeing your numbers is guessing.
  • A long contract. ⚠ Terms: Andrei to set
  • Anything you do not own. The website, the listing and the accounts are yours.
