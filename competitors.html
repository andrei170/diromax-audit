Where the searches put Diromax
Fewer reviews, higher on the map
  • “pressure washing Anglesey”: Diromax 12th. Above it: NW Exteriors (40 reviews), R.R (20), All Clean (16), Clear View (79) and seven more.
  • “gutter cleaning Holyhead”: Diromax 13th. Clear View 1st.
  • “roof cleaning Bangor Gwynedd”: Diromax not in the first 13.

⭐ With 61 five-star reviews, the reviews are not the problem. The most likely reasons are the listing's map position (about 40 miles away, near Bala), a website with no page for each service or town, and no structured data. All three are fixable.

What the leaders do that Diromax does not, yet
  • Regular customers on a schedule: Clear View takes Direct Debit for rounds, R.R sells a subscription, All Clean cleans “every 4 or 8 weeks”.
  • A page per service: NW Exteriors and All Clean both have one.
  • Instant quotes or booking: Clear View quotes online, R.R books online.
  • Tap-to-call: three of the four websites have it.

⭐ And Diromax still has one thing none of them shows on the home page: a full price list.

⚠ What this page does not contain

We did not read how much any competitor spends on ads. A Tag Manager tag shows a tool is installed, not that ads are running. On a Google search for Diromax itself, a sponsored result for another cleaning firm (EKR Cleaning) appeared on the same page.
