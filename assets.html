Questions for the call
1. How do most of your jobs reach you today: Google, Facebook, word of mouth, or repeat customers?Tells us whether the Google listing fix or a website comes first.
2. How many more jobs a week could you take on, and which service would you most like more of?A £200 roof clean and a £15 window clean need very different amounts of work to fill a week.
3. What happened with the Facebook gutter ad last winter: how many messages, and did they book?It ran for about 8 weeks and stopped on 24 January. Why it stopped decides what we change.
4. Do any customers already have you back on a schedule, for gutters or windows?If yes, that is the plan offer already proven. If no, it is the biggest untapped win.
5. When someone messages or calls while you are up a ladder, what happens to that enquiry?A text back and a short form mean you do not lose the job while you are working.
6. Who set up the Google listing, and is the address hidden?Google places it about 40 miles from Cemaes Bay. That is a ten-minute fix if you can log in.
7. The website has a franchise page and a Cheshire option on the form. Is anyone else working under Diromax today?Decides whether the site should speak for one owner or for a team.
8. How far will you travel for a job, and which towns are worth the drive?The sample site has pages for six towns. We would build the ones you actually want.
